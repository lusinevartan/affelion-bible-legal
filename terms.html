Terms of use

Affelion Bible is an English-language app for iPhone and iPad, with initial App Store availability limited to the United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand. It offers Bible readings, authored study material, prayer prompts, and a personal journal. It does not speak on God’s behalf or determine God’s will for your decisions.

Scripture and teaching

Scripture is from the World English Bible British Edition, in the public domain. It is identified separately from Affelion’s explanations and suggested prayers. Christians interpret some passages differently. The teaching is an aid to study, not a replacement for reading in context or seeking trusted counsel.

Paid access

Prayer, journal use, the complete included 66-book Bible, bookmarks, and the first study are free. The existing course purchase unlocks studies 2–7 with one payment and no automatic renewal. Its US base price is $4.99. The App Store displays the price in your local currency and applicable taxes before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple Account.

Refunds and statutory rights follow Apple’s terms and applicable local law. Restore purchases using the same Apple Account. No Affelion account is required.

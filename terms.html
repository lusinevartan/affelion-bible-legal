Care and urgent help

The app is not counselling, medical care, therapy or an emergency service. It does not diagnose or treat a condition. Seek appropriate professional help when you need it. Prayer does not require you to feel calm or stay in an unsafe situation. Your writing is not monitored by Affelion, and support email is not an emergency channel.

If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. For urgent emotional support, use the service for your location:

  • United States: call or text 988. 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.
  • Canada: call or text 9-8-8. Government of Canada crisis helpline information.
  • United Kingdom: Samaritans, 116 123. NHS urgent mental health help.
  • Australia: Lifeline, 13 11 14. Healthdirect urgent support.
  • New Zealand: call or text 1737 for brief emotional support. 1737 support and crisis information.

The app licence is Apple’s Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. That agreement applies to App Store downloads where the developer has not supplied a custom licence. This page explains Affelion’s service and support; it does not replace Apple’s agreement or reduce your legal rights.

Care and urgent help

The app is not counselling, medical care, therapy or an emergency service. It does not diagnose or treat a condition. Seek appropriate professional help when you need it. Prayer does not require you to feel calm or stay in an unsafe situation. Your writing is not monitored by Affelion, and support email is not an emergency channel.

If someone is in immediate danger, call the emergency number for their location.

  • United States
    Emergency: 911.
    988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline: call 988 or text 988.
  • Canada
    Emergency: 9-1-1.
    Suicide Crisis Helpline: call 9-8-8 or text 9-8-8.
  • United Kingdom
    Emergency: 999 or 112.
    Samaritans: call 116 123.
  • Australia
    Emergency: 000.
    Lifeline: call 13 11 14.
  • New Zealand
    Emergency: 111.
    Brief emotional support: call 1737 or text 1737. This is not an emergency response service.

Elsewhere, use your local emergency number. This list is available offline; calls and texts need a working phone connection. Affelion does not monitor your writing or contact a service for you.

Purchases, restores and refunds

This purchase does not promise access to future courses.

Apple handles App Store payments through your Apple Account. Restore purchases from the paywall or Settings using the Apple Account that bought the course. No Affelion account is needed. You can request a refund through Apple; eligibility is decided under Apple’s process and applicable law. Contact Affelion if you need help with access or wish to exercise legal rights that apply to us.

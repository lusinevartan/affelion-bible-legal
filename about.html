About Affelion Bible

Read Scripture. Make room for prayer. Practise what you learn.

The text

This build includes all 66 books and 1,189 chapters of the World English Bible British Edition for offline reading, alongside a seven-part course and prayer prompts. You can find a passage by reference, bookmark a verse, and return to your saved reading position.

Translation and public-domain notice

Our words and yours

Explanations and prayer starters are authored content, labelled separately from Scripture. Personal writing is yours. There is no chatbot generating messages from God.
